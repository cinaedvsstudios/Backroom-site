Backroom is a gay nightlife and cruising guide focused primarily on gay male and men-only nightlife across Europe, while also including a broader range of gay and queer venues and events.

Use Backroom to find gay bars, gay clubs, saunas, cruising areas, cruise bars, darkrooms, fetish and gear venues, parties, Pride events and other nightlife, with practical information to help plan where to go and what is on.

Gay nightlife across Europe

Backroom covers major gay nightlife destinations including Berlin, Hamburg, Cologne, London, Paris, Amsterdam, Brussels, Prague, Vienna, Madrid, Barcelona and Lisbon, alongside many other cities across Europe.

How the guide is built

Information is gathered through in-person visits and multiple public sources. Wherever possible, details are cross-checked against independent sources. Listings are reviewed regularly to help keep the guide useful and current.

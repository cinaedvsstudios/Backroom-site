Privacy Notice

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Backroom does not require a user account. You can browse venue and event information without providing your name or email address.

Information stored on your device

Saved venues, saved events, shortlists, travel pins, profile choices, location preferences and other settings are stored in your browser. They are not uploaded as a personal profile to Backroom. If you export or share a list, you decide where to send that information. Clearing your browser data may erase your saved settings; you can export a backup from Settings.

Venue ratings and view counts

Backroom uses Supabase to store community venue ratings and aggregate venue view totals. When you submit a rating, the website creates or reuses a randomly generated identifier held in your browser, allowing it to recognise a previous rating from that browser. The rating record associates that identifier with a venue and rating value. Venue view counts are recorded for the venue as totals rather than as named visitor profiles.

This identifier is not your name, but it can still count as pseudonymous data. Requests to third-party services may involve technical connection information, such as your IP address, handled by those services.

Contact and correction forms

If you choose to send a message, suggest a venue or report an error, the details you submit are sent through the linked external form service (including Google Forms or FormSubmit where used). Do not include sensitive information that is not needed for the request.

External services and links

Backroom is hosted using GitHub Pages and may load resources such as images, web fonts and maps from external providers. Opening external websites, map links, social profiles or ticket links is subject to those providers' own data practices.

Your choices

You can browse without rating anything. You can delete locally saved profile information using Backroom's Settings controls or by clearing the site's data in your browser. Deleting local browser data does not automatically remove rating submissions already stored in Supabase. For a privacy question or request regarding stored ratings, please contact Backroom.

Privacy and operator enquiries

Backroom is operated under the CINAEDVS Studios name. For questions about this notice, please use the contact page. A complete legal notice with the responsible operator's identity and required statutory details must be maintained separately.

This notice describes the features confirmed in the published site code. Service-side retention periods and full legal disclosures require a separate review; this page is not a certification of GDPR compliance.

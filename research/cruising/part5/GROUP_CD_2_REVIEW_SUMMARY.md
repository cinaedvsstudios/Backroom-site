## Editorial rule

A named facility's geographic coordinate proves where the named facility is, not where a specific alleged cruising section exists. Historical directories, map pins, venue operating hours, and attendance claims must be kept separate from dated positive on-site activity and from access permissions. Do not assume currently open or legal access. Do not ask the user to hand-review individual technical research questions.

Welcome to Backroom

Backroom is a gay nightlife guide covering bars, clubs, saunas, cruising venues, darkrooms, fetish events and festivals across Europe, with a focus on men-only spaces. Search by city, see what's on and save the places and nights you don't want to miss.

Explore Backroom

  • 📍 Location: Choose a city or country once; that location follows you through the venue and event calendars, festival listings and cruising directory.
  • 🔎 Search: Search venue names, event descriptions, destinations and keywords to discover something new.
  • 🏢 Venues: Browse bars, clubs, saunas, cinemas, cruising venues and shops, with details, links and available facilities.
  • 📅 Events: Select one day or several dates, choose a city and filter by party style to plan a whole weekend.
  • 🎪 Festivals: Discover grouped festival weekends, search their programmes and save individual events or whole festivals.
  • 🔥 Cruising Guide: Find public parks, beaches and other known cruising areas, with listings plus consent and safety guidance.
  • ⛓️ Terminology & Dress Codes: Browse illustrated dress codes, kink terms, gay tribes and the historical hanky-colour guide.
  • ✨ Featured: Look for the Backroom Featured badge on curated venues and selected weekly events.
  • 💖 My Events: Save nights to your personal list and keep festival events together, stored in your browser.
  • ⚜️ My Venues & My Shortlists: Save venues and combine venue and event recommendations into shareable shortlists.
  • 🚄 Travel: Pin destinations and start organising the cities you want to visit for nightlife.

Built for finding the right night

Backroom brings venue information, event calendars, festivals and cruising listings into one guide so you can compare what is happening in a destination without jumping between dozens of different sites and social accounts.

Listings are updated regularly, but schedules, entry rules and venue details can change. Check important details with the venue or organiser before travelling or buying tickets.

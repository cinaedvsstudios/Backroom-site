About this venue

El Barón Rampante is a relaxed LGBTQ-friendly café and cocktail bar just off Seville's Alameda de Hércules. Its main attraction is the outdoor terrace on Calle Arias Montano, where you can sit with a coffee, beer or cocktail and watch the neighbourhood gradually shift from afternoon to nightlife. It is next to El Bosque Animado, so the two bars make a convenient stop if you are exploring this part of the city's gay scene. Expect a mixed, sociable crowd rather than an exclusively male venue, and no dedicated cruising facilities. It suits conversation, people-watching and an unhurried first drink far better than a full-on dance night.

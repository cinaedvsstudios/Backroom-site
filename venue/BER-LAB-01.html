About this venue

Located in a former heating plant directly beneath the Berghain complex, Lab.oratory (Lab) is a dedicated men-only sex and fetish club. It operates independently from the main club upstairs and is not a standard dance venue. ENTRY & QUEUE Getting there Access the queue via Am Wriezener Bahnhof. The line forms down the alley. Choose the correct line The queue splits near the venue. The line for Lab turns left. The line on the right is for Berghain. Make sure you join the correct queue: the gate closest to the door is often locked, so you cannot cross between them. No internal Berghain access There is no internal access to Berghain through Lab. To visit the main club afterwards, leave Lab and join the regular Berghain queue. ENTRY & CHECK-IN Door times Entry is limited to a specific two-hour window at the start of the night, for example 20:00–22:00. Once the door closes, no further entry is allowed. People already waiting in the queue are generally processed. Wristband and bag system After entry and bag check, you receive a numbered wristband. This number is your bar tab for the night. You also receive a plastic bag marked with the same number for your street clothes at the cloakroom. Checking out Before leaving, collect your clothes and go to the counter to scan your wristband and pay your bar tab. You receive a receipt, which must be handed to door staff when exiting. INSIDE THE VENUE Atmosphere Lab is not designed for casual socialising, group hangouts, or conventional romantic behaviour. Play areas In the darkrooms and designated cruise areas, keep the atmosphere quiet and focused. · No talking or singing · No smoking · No phone lights

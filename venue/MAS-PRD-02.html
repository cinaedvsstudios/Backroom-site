About this venue

Winter Pride brings a second burst of Pride energy to Maspalomas in November, when much of northern Europe is already reaching for a coat. Expect free evening concerts and drag shows on the Yumbo main stage, plus ticketed pool parties, boat trips, sports, excursions and the Pride Walk. The crowd is international and the programme mixes big nights out with plenty of daytime socialising. You don't need to attend every event to enjoy the atmosphere around Yumbo, but the popular pool parties are worth planning ahead for. It's more festival holiday than single-night club event, so check the day-by-day programme before choosing your dates.

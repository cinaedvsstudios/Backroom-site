About this venue

San Donato is a neighbourhood park in Florence's Novoli district, rather than a secluded all-night cruising ground. A gay directory includes it as a possible meeting place, but there is little support for the more ambitious claims about late-night visits. One visitor in July 2025 said the gates closed around 20:30–21:00. Florence's municipality reported further restrictions and fenced paths during repair works in June 2026. Neither development confirms any recent cruising activity. If you're visiting the park for its green space, follow the currently posted access hours and avoid areas closed for works. As a cruising recommendation it lacks what matters most: a current firsthand report, an identifiable meeting section, reliable times and an idea of the crowd.
